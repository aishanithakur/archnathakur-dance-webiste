About The Mentor

Archna Thakur is a passionate dance instructor and Kathak artist with over 30 years of Kathak practice and performance experience. She has graduated in Kathak (Prabhakar) from Prayag Sangeet Samiti, Allahabad, and has been dedicated to promoting the art of dance through teaching, choreography and cultural performances. With more than 14 years of teaching experience, she trains students across various age groups including kids, teenagers and adults. Specializing in Kathak, Semi-Classical and Bollywood dance, she also incorporates vibrant regional dance forms Her dedication towards dance education has earned her prestigious recognitions including the Global Sustainability Award (GSA) 2025 and 9th Dr. Sarojini Naidu International Award for Working Women (2025), organized by ICMEI, IWFF, AAFT University and Marwah Studios. Archna continues to inspire students to grow artistically while building discipline, confidence and stage presence.
